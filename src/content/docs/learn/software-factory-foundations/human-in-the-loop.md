People set the scope and acceptance criteria, respond to exceptions, and make the review or release decisions required by the project. Employees execute authorized work and provide results and evidence.

## Recognize a changed decision

While correcting the form message, the employee discovers that solving the request may require changing a shared validation rule. That could affect other forms. The original scope no longer settles the next action: ask the responsible person to confirm the wider change or choose a smaller solution.

If a person takes over work, record the intervention and explicitly hand it back before expecting automatic progress. The available continuation or retry action depends on the current record and product flow.

## Try this judgment

A person has taken over a blocked card. Someone later resumes a pause. Has automatic control necessarily returned?

Takeover and pause are separate controls. Check the current state and explicitly hand work back when it is ready to continue.

Use [Human approval](/user-guide/human-approval/) for those decisions and [Review and release](/software-factory/review-release/) for the project's later stages.
