## Review results and keep work moving

Use [artifacts and evidence](/user-guide/artifacts-evidence/) to understand what an employee produced and how to verify it. For engineering delivery, the [Software Factory](/software-factory/overview/) connects requirements confirmation, execution, verification, and human review in the workbench.

When a result needs another pass, return a specific observation and an expected outcome. [Learn](/learn/) covers requirements and feedback methods; [Developers](/developer/) covers the employee packages, tools, and integrations behind the work.

If execution stops or a connection fails, use [troubleshooting](/resources/troubleshooting/) to collect the relevant state, then check [releases and support](/resources/releases-support/) for the next step.
