**AI Workforce is an enterprise platform for AI collaboration and software delivery.** It brings professional digital employees, people, project knowledge, and engineering systems into one path from requirements through development, testing, security, and release. Teams can define a role with an FDE, verify it on sample tasks, and put it to work in a real project. [Follow a role from interview to first delivery](/build/).

The platform combines professional digital employees, a delivery workbench, and an enterprise runtime. It manages sessions, tool permissions, execution environments, integrations, and evidence so teams can continuously improve how software is delivered. ## From a delivery signal to verified software

Follow Story #128 on the board below to see how role selection for workspace invitations went from a research finding to a change deployed for testing.

[![An AI Workforce delivery board with three columns. The team approves a competitor research finding, adds it to the backlog as Story #128, and tracks its GitHub Actions results.](/illustrations/delivery-board-en.svg)](/illustrations/delivery-board-en.html)

[Explore the interactive delivery board](/illustrations/delivery-board-en.html)

1. **Collect the finding.** A scheduled research Job reads a competitor’s release notes, spots role selection in the invite flow, and sends the finding and its source to the Task inbox. [Set up a scheduled Job](/automate/triggers-schedules/)
2. **Review and add to Backlog.** A team member reviews the finding in the inbox and routes it to the delivery project as Story #128. Before development, the team confirms the scope and acceptance criteria on the card. [Review a request and confirm its scope](/user-guide/human-approval/)
3. **Verify the change.** In the Delivery tab, PR #62 links to GitHub Actions run #482. The build and tests passed and the change reached the test environment. Production release is still pending. [Connect your CI/CD pipeline](/software-factory/cicd/)
