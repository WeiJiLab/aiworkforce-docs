A workspace is the shared context for working with AI employees. It keeps related employees and resources together so you can find the right worker and inspect the work associated with it.

## Open and organize a workspace

Open **Workspaces**, then select the workspace you want to use. The workspace entry leads to conversations with its employees. Workspace management contains **Digital employees** (数字员工) and **Task inbox** (任务收件箱).

Use one workspace for a coherent area of work. Choose a descriptive name and make sure members understand its purpose. Creating many spaces with overlapping responsibilities makes it harder to know where to find the current employee or request.

## Manage employees and resources

On **Digital employees**, choose **Add digital employee** and create a worker from a published template. **Marketplace** only browses templates. Review the template's purpose, configure the options offered, and wait for initialization before using it.

Configure connections and repository resources in the appropriate workspace settings. A connection must exist and be authorized for the intended use before an employee can access it. Workspace resource setup does not replace the explicit employee authorization required by a delivery workflow.

## Understand access

What you can see and what you can change depend on your workspace permissions. A user who can read a workspace may not be able to create employees, configure connections, or execute work. Ask the workspace creator, a team leader, or an administrator to change the grant for a concrete task. See [Teams and access](/administration/teams-access/).

Avoid sharing credentials in conversations. Use the product's configured connection and authorization flows instead. Give an employee the smallest set of resources needed to complete its responsibility.

## Review workspace work

Use the **Task inbox** to review incoming requests, their sources, and their current states. Use employee sessions to inspect conversations and outputs. For coordinated development, open **Workbench** (工作台) and select the relevant project or board. The address is `/automations?tab=board-flow`.

A useful workspace has employees with clear responsibilities, resources that match those responsibilities, and an agreed review process for outputs.

## Troubleshooting

If an employee or item is missing, confirm that you opened the correct workspace and have access. If a management action is unavailable, check your permissions. If execution cannot reach a resource, inspect both the connection and the employee's authorization.
