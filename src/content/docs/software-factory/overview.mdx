Software Factory connects a confirmed engineering request to a change your team can verify and review. It uses your project's repositories, workers, and engineering environment. Each delivery card keeps the requirement, execution, code reference, checks, and decisions connected.

## How a change moves forward

<ProcessDiagram id="delivery-loop" />

A failed check returns evidence to development. After repair, verification must cover the new revision. People confirm scope, handle exceptions, and make the review and release decisions required by the project.

The **delivery workbench**, opened from **Workbench** (工作台) in the primary navigation at `/automations?tab=board-flow`, is where you follow this work. The [Kanban workbench](/workflows/kanban-workbench/) describes that same board; [Issue automation](/workflows/issue-automation/) is one way requests enter it.

## What supports the delivery

**Ready resources.** The project needs a board, repository connections, ready lane employees, and the environment or pipeline for the checks you plan to run. Repository credentials belong to the workspace; each employee needs its own grant. Confirm provider, instance, enabled state, and workspace when selecting the connection. See [environments](/software-factory/environments/) and [code repositories](/integrations/code-repositories/).

**Linked execution.** A confirmed card is assigned to the lane employee. Sessions show the work performed; batches group related cards when later stages test or release them together. See [task dispatch](/software-factory/dispatch/) and [agent orchestration](/software-factory/orchestration/).

**Reviewable evidence.** Checks identify the revision, environment, and result. A reviewer compares them with the acceptance criteria and records acceptance, a return for repair, or an explicit waiver. See [build and test](/software-factory/build-test/), [evidence](/software-factory/evidence/), and [quality gates](/software-factory/quality-gates/).

Creating a card does not prepare these resources. The configured runtime and harness determine how a worker executes; see [coding agents and harnesses](/software-factory/harness/) for current support. The board does not supply a per-card sandbox or built-in build farm, and business acceptance requires a recorded comparison by the team.

## Keep human decisions and release outcomes explicit

Requirements must be confirmed before development. Answer questions and approval requests on the card. Taking over stops automatic progress and preserves its state; explicitly hand the work back to continue. Resuming a pause does not clear a takeover.

Built-in boards use Requirements, Development, Test deployment, Testing, Production, and Done. Custom boards may differ. Lane steps describe execution state, and operations on a shared batch can affect every member card. There is no WIP-limit control. The [Kanban workbench](/workflows/kanban-workbench/) covers board behavior.

Merge and production release require working repository and pipeline integrations. Inspect merge, deployed revision, and cleanup separately; closing a batch does not prove that release or resource cleanup succeeded. Follow [review and release](/software-factory/review-release/) for those records.

Use [workflow concepts](/workflows/concepts/) when you need to connect the board to Jobs and lane pipelines. Use [evaluation and governance](/operate/evaluation-governance/) when improving worker behavior across repeated tasks.
