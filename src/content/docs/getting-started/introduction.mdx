AI Workforce helps enterprise engineering teams turn work in existing projects into results they can accept. AI workers use authorized tools and engineering environments; people agree on the scope and review the evidence. Start with a small change, then reuse methods that prove useful on real tasks.

## How the pieces fit

A **workflow** describes how work is performed. **Automation** starts it manually or from an available trigger. An **AI worker**, shown as an employee in the workspace, performs the assigned steps. A run records what happened, and evidence supports the review decision.

Builders turn procedures into reusable employee packages. Published templates let teams create workspace employees. The runtime platform runs tasks with their context and authorizations. FDEs and teams use task results to refine the procedures and evaluate later versions; this improvement also involves human work.

For software delivery, the delivery board keeps the confirmed requirement, implementation, verification, and decisions together. For a task that does not change software, you can [use an employee](/getting-started/first-employee/) and review its output, then [automate recurring work](/getting-started/first-automation/) after a manual run succeeds.

Use [core concepts](/getting-started/core-concepts/) when you need the vocabulary and the [platform map](/getting-started/platform-overview/) when you need to locate a screen.
