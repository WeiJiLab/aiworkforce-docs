AI Workforce helps engineering teams turn requests into changes they can verify and review. [Software Factory](/software-factory/overview/) gives each change a card on the delivery workbench. The card links the confirmed requirement, the implementation, the verification evidence, and the team's decision.

[Workflow automation](/automate/fundamentals/) starts recurring or event-driven work. For example, a scheduled Job can collect defect reports for triage. A person confirms the scope and acceptance criteria on the delivery card before implementation. AI employees perform the work assigned to them on the board and return evidence for review.

## Choose your path

- **Deliver your first change.** Follow the [quickstart](/getting-started/quickstart/) to turn one Issue into a reviewed change. Use [Issue to PR](/recipes/issue-to-pr/) for a worked example.
- **Automate intake or recurring work.** Try [your first automation](/getting-started/first-automation/) to inspect a manual run before enabling a trigger.
- **Set up a delivery project.** [Configure the board](/getting-started/first-software-factory/) with repository access and verification resources. If a lane needs an employee, [create one from a template](/getting-started/first-employee/) or [define a reusable role](/build/employee-definition/).
- **Review and release changes.** Use [review and release](/software-factory/review-release/) to follow verification, merge, and deployment for a configured project.

## Before you begin

You need an AI Workforce account and access to a workspace. For a delivery workflow, choose a workbench project with a repository connection, ready lane employees, and a way to verify the change. Ask a workspace administrator to help with access or configuration. This documentation marks partial support as **Limited** and future capabilities as **Planned**.

## What success looks like

For your first change, a reviewer can open the confirmed requirement, identify the revision that was checked, read the result, and see whether the team accepted the change or returned it for repair. Start with one small Issue with clear acceptance criteria.
